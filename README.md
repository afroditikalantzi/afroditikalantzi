# 👋 Hi, I'm Afroditi Kalantzi

### Design Engineer working across digital and physical systems

I'm a design engineer, and what I care about most is the space where design thinking and technology overlap. Whatever the project is, I tend to work the same way, sketch it out, get a rough version working fast, put it in front of real people, and fix what doesn't land.

Some of that ends up as a screen, an ecommerce platform, a data driven web app for managing performer profiles. Some of it ends up as something you can actually hold, an AR guide built for a museum, an autonomous vessel tested in open water, a CAD model turned into a real jet engine. For my diploma thesis, I built a system that projects live ship data onto a physical 3D model of the Cyclades, controlled entirely through hand gestures, still probably the project that best sums up how I like to work.

This comes from how my degree is built. It's an integrated master's in Product and Systems Design Engineering from the University of the Aegean, and it blends interactive systems, computer aided design, and the more systemic side of design, understanding services and complex systems as a whole. That mix is why the range never really narrowed to one lane.

I'm currently looking for remote work, open to frontend, backend, product design, technical support, or something else entirely, whether that means one of those on its own or a mix of a few.
